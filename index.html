FAQ

Questions about
the questions.

17 benchmarks covering general knowledge (MMLU), reasoning (HellaSwag, SWAG, COPA), science (ARC, SciQ, QASC), math (MathQA, AQUA-RAT), medicine (MedMCQA), reading comprehension (RACE), and more. Over 90,000 questions total.

All questions come from official, open-source research datasets — the same ones used to evaluate frontier AI models. The data is cleaned, deduplicated, and bundled locally with the app for instant offline-ready loading. No external APIs are used.

Exactly the same way AI models are scored — percentage of correct answers out of total answered. 50 random questions per session. Your score is plotted alongside GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V3.2, Llama 4 Maverick, and Grok 4.2.

Yes. All AI model scores are from published benchmark results and standardized evaluations. They represent each model's actual performance on these specific test sets.
